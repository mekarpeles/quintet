#+TITLE: Quintet — The Pitch
#+SUBTITLE: 2026-09-02

* Note

Written as a spoken pitch. It takes decisions on several open gaps in order to
be sayable -- flagged below the pitch. Revisit if any decision is wrong.

* The pitch

Every house in Orbis has the same book on the shelf. A home remedy manual, four
hundred years old, hand-copied so many times that no two are alike.

The Red Shepherds have just published the authorized edition. Bring your copy to
the square. If it matches, take it home.

Leina Grey is twelve. Her mother's copy does not match.

Nume Grey was a healer once, pushed out of the profession for being impossible to
work with. Everyone in Blackmoor thinks she is a lazy madwoman who reads too much
and gave up on her daughter. What she actually is, is the only person in the
province who understands what the Reds are really doing -- and she cannot tell a
soul, because it is not merely dangerous to say. It is insane to say.

So she does the only thing left. She puts it in her daughter.

Twelve years of drilling. Six languages. Recall tests before dawn. Not an
education -- a backup. And the instruction that comes with it: /be nobody./
Finish your schooling adequately. Take a dull placement. Be written off. A hidden
thing is only safe while it is hidden.

Leina executes it perfectly, hates every hour of it, and cannot dismiss it,
because the logic keeps turning out to be right.

Then a man with a failing heart is sent to collect the book, and kills her mother
because she is standing between him and the paperwork.

Now Leina is the only copy, with nobody left alive to read her, and being nobody
has stopped being survivable.

And every single thing she does to make herself safe makes her a larger target.
That is the series.

She survives the only way left: by making more copies of herself. That is the
Academy. That is how she wins.

And it is the one thing her mother -- who trusted no one -- could never have
conceived.

* Decisions this pitch takes

- *The Reds arrive in Westhaven during Act I.* The square-and-table edition check
  comes to town. Act I is hot, not theoretical. (Resolves gap 1.)
- *What Nume knows is not a weapon, it is a deadline.* She has correctly worked
  out the Protector's method and roughly how long is left. She is not holding a
  key -- she is holding the problem, stated accurately, for the first time.
  Nobody would believe her, which is precisely why she reads as mad. (Resolves
  gap 3, and avoids the plot-coupon problem.)
- *Nume's profession was healer / Soul Speaker*, and she was pushed out of it.
  (Partially resolves gap 2 -- the institution still needs naming.)
- *Leina is an Oracle by the world's definition.* She does not know the word
  applies to her. (Resolves gap 6.)

Still open: Alistair, Reygen, the geography of the Oracle, Wick's status.
