#+TITLE: Quintet — Prologue
#+SUBTITLE: Opening voiceover

* Note

Fellowship-style cold open: a voice over images, the myth and the threat, then
narrowing to the smallest possible thing.

- Draft 1: loss-by-attrition. Superseded -- it was persecution, not decay.
- Draft 2: fixed that, but all condition and no story. No objects, nothing to see.
- Draft 3: tracked a single sacred book through four hundred years. Superseded --
  too Ring-like. There is no one true text and no chain of martyrs holding one
  artifact.
- Draft 4 (rev. 2026-09-02): the Manual is a book in *every* house. Square-check
  cut per mek -- the Reds do not sweep, they ask, and they take the wrong people
  for years. Protector passage rewritten: no pronoun ever (the title is the
  pronoun), no origin, no "stood at the same wall." Lead with the stone. Divergence is the crime. The
  image is a town square, a table, and a queue of people holding their family's
  copy.

Voice: unattributed, reads as a woman's.

* Draft 4

Every house has one.

It sits on the shelf between the almanac and the accounts, and it comes down when
a child spikes a fever or a ewe won't drop her lamb. The Soul Speaker's Manual.
Your mother had one. Hers did. You have never read it front to back and you don't
need to -- you know the six pages that matter, the way you know a prayer you
stopped believing.

Every child in Orbis can recite the opening couplet. Most of them do it without
hearing it.

#+begin_quote
The Oracle is Death, where souls speak no more.
Your final breath she seals, beneath the Orbis core.
#+end_quote

Nobody knows who wrote the thing. That was never the interesting question.

The interesting question is why yours is not the same as your neighbour's.

Four hundred years of copying by hand. Four hundred years of tired men who
couldn't quite read a word and chose one. And here and there, in the space at the
edge of the page where nothing important is ever written -- somebody who tried it,
and found it didn't work, and set down what did.

Not truth. Nobody has the truth. There is no perfect book and there never was.
There is only the black stone, and the few born able to feel the life in things,
and four centuries of ordinary people making notes in the margins and being
wrong.

Mostly.

The ones who were not wrong were called Orackai, and we hanged them. Not all at
once -- there is never a single purge. A bad winter. A suspicious death. A lord
who found one of them inconvenient. Some of them were burned in the square to
make the point, and their books with them, and their students learned to keep
very quiet.

And now there are the Reds.

They do not search. Searching is for people who do not know what they are looking
for. They ask, and they are patient, and every few years somebody's book turns out
to be the one -- and that somebody is taken north and is not seen again, and then
the book turns out not to be the one after all.

So they widen it.

In the north there is a thing called the Protector.

The Protector was a person once. Nobody agrees when, or which one, and it does
not matter. What walks now is Oracle Stone, held in one piece by flesh and
vitriol -- and the Protector is not mad, and does not rage, and does not lie to
you, because lying is a courtesy you extend to someone whose behaviour still
matters to you.

No one holds the black stone now but the Protector, who keeps it safe on our
behalf. Every life the Protector takes goes down into the dark, and the dark grows louder
for it. There is no plan here that you could ruin. There is nothing wanted that
you could withhold.

And the Protector is popular. That is the part that should frighten you. Because
the promise is that there will never be another age of Orackai -- and the
Protector finds them, and takes them, and they are not seen again, and ordinary
people sleep better.

The Protector is very close now.

And in a fishing province at the wet edge of nowhere, where nobody is anybody and
everybody is content to stay that way, there is a woman whose book is the
one they are looking for.

She has a daughter who is five years old.

She does not know how much time is left.

So she is going to be cruel.
