#+TITLE: Leina and Ardovus — the arc, as events
#+SUBTITLE: Drafted 2026-09-17. Specific incidents, roughly chronological.

* Mechanics this assumes

- The bind is *active*, not passive: Leina carries the load continuously and can
  let go. Letting go does not kill her; it kills him over hours or days, and it is
  one-way.
- Range is irrelevant. That is the trap — he can leave whenever he likes and spend
  the rest of his life waiting for his chest to stop.
- It runs both directions. Her pain, exhaustion and fear come through to him.
- He appears to be in charge for a long time, and she lets him, because a man who
  feels in control does not panic.

* Phase one — the first ten days

- *He offers to deal with the body.* Professionally, and he is not being cruel
  about it; it is the single most useful thing he can do. She refuses and buries
  Nume herself. It takes her until four in the morning and she does it badly.
- He waits, without comment, the entire time. Then at dawn, while she sleeps, he
  re-digs it properly and stacks the stones. Neither of them ever mentions it.
- *He ties her at night.* Three nights. She does not sleep tied, and on the fourth
  morning he unties her and does not explain — because her exhaustion has been
  coming through to him and he worked it out before she thought to argue it. His
  first concession, made for selfish reasons, won without a word.
- *His maintenance date passes.* Eleven years, never missed. He sits up all that
  night waiting for it and nothing happens. She watches him not have an attack and
  says nothing at all, which is the correct play and costs her something.
- He does not send word. He tells himself it is because he has not finished
  assessing. It is not.

* Phase two — the learning period

- *In towns he drags her by the arm*, performing an uncle with a difficult niece.
  She plays the sulking child extremely well. It is also useful: people look away
  from a man hauling a girl, so nobody ever looks at her face.
- *She begins asking small questions.* "How do you know he's a courier and not a
  farmer?" Each one is reasonable and answerable, and he answers, and over nine
  weeks she reconstructs his entire method without once asking for it.
- *She catches an informant he missed* at an inn on the Antry road — a man who
  orders and does not drink. She mentions it as though it is nothing. He moves them
  that night. It is the first time he acts on her judgement and they both file it.
- *The overpaid room.* She tells him he paid a third over and could have had it
  cheaper by arriving after the boats. He ignores her. Two towns later she is
  proved right in front of him, and she does not press it, which is worse.
- *He teaches her to fight* — nothing like Lemeus. No forms. Where to put a thumb,
  how to fall, which bones are cheap. He teaches her to run properly, which she
  resents, and which will save her twice.
- *She has people.* Four days into a river town he notices a boy runs messages for
  her and a woman sells them bread under price, and he cannot work out when she
  arranged any of it.

* Phase three — the chips come off

- *The cut.* She injures herself on a pump handle, deliberately, and watches his
  face. He says nothing for eight days. Then, one night, across a fire: "You're
  running an experiment on me." She says yes. She does not apologise, and does not
  stop.
- That is the first time he is genuinely frightened of her — not of dying, which he
  is used to, but of her *willingness.*
- *He gets sick with something ordinary.* A wound goes bad. She holds him together
  for three days and it nearly finishes her, and he watches what it costs her from
  the inside. First time the price is visible to him rather than theoretical.
- *He kills a man to protect her.* Quickly, correctly, and without discussion. She
  is grateful, and is appalled at herself for being grateful, and never mentions
  either.
- *The Reds start asking after him* — five or six weeks overdue. There is a
  description circulating. He has to decide, out loud, in front of her, to run
  rather than report. First irreversible act.
- *The money flips.* She funds them. He notices on the day she buys the horse, and
  what he feels is not gratitude, it is vertigo.
- *She stops performing the sulking child.* No announcement. She simply doesn't do
  it one morning, and he finds he doesn't ask her to.

* Phase four — symbiosis, and Odile

- *He starts telling her things she did not ask for.* The hierarchy. The
  maintenance stations. Names. That is the tell, and she notices it before he does.
- *She gives him a direction.* She has read the casebook properly and says they are
  going north to Coldhurst. She does not make a case for it. He goes.
- *Coldhurst.* Leina pretends not to know Odile, flawlessly, four feet from him.
  Ardovus watches her lie to a stranger and understands, watching, that it is not
  a stranger.
- *Afterwards he asks who the woman was.* And Leina lies to him — properly, for the
  first time in months. And he lets it go.
- Which is the moment his position changes, and he is the only one in the room who
  fully understands why: she has an outside now. Somewhere else to go. Until
  Coldhurst he was the only thing standing between her and the world, and that was
  worth something, and now it is worth less.
