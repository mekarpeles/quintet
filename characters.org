* Character Notes

These are living notes. They capture our current understanding and may evolve.

**Last Updated:** 2026-04-08
**Leina's Age:** 12 at start of Book I. 14 at end.

---

* Leina Grey

**The Protagonist.** Age 12 at start of Book I, turning 14 by end. Lives in Blackmoor, a wooded hamlet outside Westhaven. Extraordinary competence, not extraordinary ability. Her brilliance is Nume's design, not fate.

**Core Wound:** Her mother was right. Every brutal lesson. Every paranoid fear. Every impossible demand. The world is exactly as dangerous as Nume said. Leina survived because her mother broke her in exactly the right ways. She cannot forgive this. She cannot resent this cleanly. The love and the damage are fused.

**The Contradiction She Lives:**
- Wants solitude, peace, freedom from politics → builds an academy and leads a resistance
- Trusts no one → people gather around her anyway, and she cannot refuse them
- Sees herself as a manipulator → her refusal to politic is precisely what people trust
- Learns connection is necessary → her first genuine connection is betrayed, proving Nume right about something

**How She Was Made:**
Nume's pedagogy was not grooming for admiration. It was survival preparation wrapped in control, wrapped in love. Leina internalized it. The learning, the questioning, the relentless competence — she made it her own. It became identity, not just survival. The distance from Nume felt like freedom. But it was just a different cage.

**The Ardovus Dynamic:**
Ardovus represents a new context for Leina's skills. Not just survival against systems, but survival against someone with leverage. She must be better than him. She must outthink him. The Eye is not abstract anymore — it has a face, and it is watching her specifically.

**What People Follow in Her:**
Not what she intends. She has no pitch. No vision. No movement-building strategy. People follow her because she sees clearly. Because she refuses to pretend. Because her walls — the distance, the coldness, the refusal to perform — are made of something people need to stand behind. Safety through honesty. Not safety through lies.

**Her Fatal Flaw:**
Believes strategy can substitute for connection. This nearly destroys her. Repeatedly.

**Practical Notes:**
- Wears Oracle Stone necklace (large trillion cut)
- Uncle Haesius Moore (Nume's brother) lives alone; Leina moved in without discussion
- Has a younger brother, Reygen (see: Character Gaps)
- Works with Dr. Yitna (herbalist) collecting wild herbs
- Invests grain through Mrs. Cudner
- Delegated to Melinda Sauveneux (loyal girl, treated as sister)
- Reads military tactics, polyglot dictionaries

* Nume Moore Grey

**Leina's mother.** Dead before or at the story's opening. Sharp nose. Curly dark unkempt hair. Manic-depressive. Polyglot. Blacksmith. An eclectic collector of annotated books.

**Core Truth:** She deeply loved her daughter and would do anything for her. Her intent never translated. Her character flaws consumed the delivery. She could not hold love without crushing it.

**Why She Was Right:**
Not paranoid — prescient. Every fear was validated. The Red Shepherds are real. The Eye is real. Leina survived because Nume broke her in exactly the right ways. This is the cruelest thing about her: her methods were monstrous, and they worked.

**The Unresolvable:**
Leina can never say thank you. She can never argue back. She can never know if the love was real or if she's reconstructing it to survive the damage. The answer is probably both. They always are.

**What She Left Behind:**
- Annotated Soul Seeker's Manual (now with Leina)
- Secret chest in her home, contents unknown
- A key (found on her body) that unlocks a box at Alistair's grave — which contains a key to her chest
- Annotations in the Manual — many written by Alistair, not her (Leina won't know this until later)

**Her Death:**
Killed by Ardovus Retik. Not a sacrifice. Not a choice. She was simply in the way. This randomness is important — Nume did not save Leina. She was found first. Leina arrived to find only the body.

**The Question She Leaves:**
Why was she so afraid? What did she know about the Reds, the Manual, Alistair? The answers are in the chest. Leina will find them. They will not bring peace.

* Ardovus Retik

**The Assassin.** Sent by the Red Shepherds to locate annotated copies of the Soul Seeker's Manual. Has a fatal heart condition. His life depends on the Reds' Soul Seekers keeping him alive — and on acquiring the Manual.

**The Coercion:**
He is not ideological. He is not evil. He is a man with a failing heart who was told the Reds can save him. Everything he does is survival. This makes him more dangerous than a true believer — he has no cause to betray, no principle to resist.

**What He Finds in Leina:**
At first: a target. A tool. A resource to be leveraged. But Leina is not what he expected. She sees through him. She knows his weakness. She binds their fates in a moment of desperation, and he is forced to see her as something other than a mission.

**The Codependency:**
He needs her. She needs him. Neither chose this. The bond is not romantic — it is something stranger. Survival. Mutual hostage. Eventually, something neither of them names.

**What He Knows:**
He knows about Alistair Grey. He knows the Grey family is significant. This is information Leina does not have — and it will shape how she understands her own history.

**Practical Notes:**
- Disciplined (waited three nights in rain for a target)
- Choosy about targets (would not kill children casually)
- Can be deterred by pain (bitten by Leina in their first fight)
- Knows the Reds' methods, their hierarchy, their weaknesses

* Nume's Brother (Unnamed in current draft — Haesius Moore)

Leina's maternal uncle. Currently named Haesius Moore in some drafts. Lives alone. Leina moved in without discussion. A pushover. Scarred by growing up with Nume. He provides shelter but not guidance. Leina respects his silence.

**Narrative Function:**
He represents the choice Leina did not make — staying in the family, accepting the orbit of Nume's legacy without fighting it. He is what Leina would become if she surrendered to the exhaustion of being exceptional.

**What He Knows:**
He was there. He watched Nume raise Leina. He may know things about Alistair, about the Manual, about the chest. But he will not speak easily. Leina will have to ask the right questions.

---

* Alistair Grey

**Leina's father.** Likely dead or unhelpful. Left writings behind. The annotations in the Soul Seeker's Manual are from his hand — Leina won't recognize this until later. Famous. His reputation (good or bad?) is unclear.

**The Debt:**
Whatever Alistair was, whatever he did, his actions left something on Leina's shoulders. Not monetary. Not explained. Not even clearly understood by Alistair himself. He understood politicking — he made promises, compromises, moved pieces on the chessboard. He made deals with people whose names Leina doesn't know. His intentions were probably unclear even to himself. His ultimate goals, the factors he was contending with, whether his plans panned out — all of this is fog. Net positive or negative? Unknown. He may have thought he was helping. He may have thought he was saving the world. The debris of his machinations is what Leina inherits.

**What Ardovus Knows:**
He recognizes the Grey name. He knows something. This is a thread Leina cannot pull until the right moment.

**Reygen's Possible Fate:**
Not confirmed. Possibly with Alistair. Open question. (See: Character Gaps)

**Narrative Function:**
He is the missing piece. Nume never speaks of him. He is not in the home. He left — or was removed. The chest, the key, the annotations — they all point toward him. He is the mystery at the center of the mystery. The question is not whether he was good or evil. The question is what promises he made and whether Leina will have to keep them.

---

* Johan Lemeus

**Neighbor and reluctant mentor.** Former Colonel in the Westhaven Guard. Former swordsman. Having an affair with Lindy Abigale. Farmer by trade. Good man, weak will.

**Narrative Function:**
He represents ordinary competence being pulled into Leina's orbit without understanding why. He is the first adult to take her seriously — and she terrifies him. Their training sessions are where Leina learns that her body is a limitation, not just her mind.

---

* The Protector

**Leader of the Red Shepherds.** Never named. Never fully seen. The faceless authority. The Eye. He believes in forced unity, assimilation, human potential perfected. His conviction is real. His methods are monstrous. His goal: immortality and the strength to conquer.

**What He Wants:**
Not destruction. Completion. He believes the Reds are completing humanity's potential. The suffering is transitional. The cost is acceptable.

**What He Fears:**
The same thing every tyrant fears — obsolescence. Being replaced. Being forgotten. His immortality project is not about living forever. It is about ensuring his *meaning* lives forever.

* Notes and Questions

**On the Bond:**
The soul-bond between Leina and Ardovus must have physical/mystical rules we haven't defined. How long can they be apart? What does separation feel like? When Ardovus dies at the end, what happens to Leina? We need to establish these mechanics clearly in Book I so the stakes feel grounded.
